Privacy Policy Support

Need help with Zombddon? Email m.s.doroshenko@gmail.com. The developer is llkenny. You can also copy the address into your preferred email service.

Report a problem

To help investigate, please include:

  • Your game version (available in iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Zombddon).
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The contract and the steps that led to the problem.
  • What you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot is optional.

Do not include passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information. Read the Privacy Policy for how voluntary support email is handled. Response times are not guaranteed.

Driving and aiming

Hold and move the left joystick to steer and accelerate. Release it to brake. Hold and move the right joystick to aim the mounted weapons together. Weapons fire automatically; releasing the right joystick lets each mounted weapon aim at its nearest visible enemy in range. A centered held touch keeps the last manual aim direction.

Contracts and extraction

Choose an unlocked contract and press LET'S RIDE. Reach its kill quota, then follow the extraction compass and stay in the extraction zone for two seconds. Missions 1–9 also allow victory by clearing all enemies, including reserves. The final mission requires defeating the boss before extraction.

Sound

MUSIC and EFFECTS are independent switches in Contracts and Pause. Music plays in menus, not during missions. If sound is missing, check these switches, device volume, the iPhone silent setting, and your audio output. The game respects silent mode.

Pause and retry

Use the pause button to stop the mission. Switching away from the app also pauses it; press RESUME after returning. RETRY starts a fresh attempt, resetting the car, weapons, upgrades, enemies, and pickups. Saved contract and partner unlocks remain.

Progress and partners

Contract unlocks, unlocked and selected partners, and audio preferences are stored locally. Rescue a partner and win that partner's mission to unlock selection. An unfinished attempt is not restored after the app closes.

There is no developer-hosted cloud save or account recovery service. Deleting the app can remove progress; offloading or restoring a system backup behaves according to your device settings. Recovery of lost or deleted progress cannot be guaranteed. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep local progress.

Offline play and these pages

The game and the bundled support and privacy pages work offline. Sending email or opening the public website requires the relevant external service and an internet connection. If no mail app can handle the email link, copy the displayed address into your preferred email service.

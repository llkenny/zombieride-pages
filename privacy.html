Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

Privacy Policy Support

Zombddon is an offline iPhone game. Playing does not require an account. The game does not send your gameplay data to the developer.

Developer and contact

Zombddon is maintained by llkenny. For privacy questions or requests, email m.s.doroshenko@gmail.com.

Information stored by the game

The game stores campaign unlocks, unlocked and selected partners, and music and effects preferences locally on your device. This information is used to restore your progress and preferences when you reopen the game.

The app does not operate a developer-hosted cloud save service. Depending on your device settings, Apple-managed device or iCloud backups may include app data. Those backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

No in-game collection or tracking

Zombddon has no user accounts, advertising, tracking, in-game analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect or transmit your gameplay data to the developer, sell personal information, or request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Apple may process platform diagnostics under your device settings and Apple's own policies. This is separate from data collection implemented by Zombddon.

Optional support email

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you include, such as a description of a problem or a screenshot. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.

This information is used to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, and improve the game. Email is handled through Gmail and the email providers involved in delivery; see Google's Privacy Policy. Support correspondence is not used for advertising or sold.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until removed. Deleting the app removes the data from that installation; offloading the app may preserve it. Copies in system backups are managed separately through your Apple and device settings. The developer cannot access or delete your device backups.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request access to or deletion of your support correspondence by emailing m.s.doroshenko@gmail.com. The developer may need to verify that the request concerns your correspondence, and may retain information where required by law.

Contacting support is optional. You may stop corresponding at any time without affecting your ability to play. There is no game account to delete and no in-game tracking consent to withdraw.

These public pages

The bundled pages can be read inside the app without an internet connection. They have no scripts, forms, analytics, cookies set by these pages, or external fonts and images.

If you open the public website, it is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses and request information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an external website or your mail app is your choice and uses that service's own privacy settings and policies.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The effective date appears above. The app includes the version shipped with that build; the published version is available through the link below.
